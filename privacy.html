Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Gameworthy ("the app", "we") tells you which games are worth watching without showing you the score. It has no accounts and no server of its own. What you set up stays on your phone. This page says what the app saves, what leaves your phone and what you control.

The short version

  • No account and no sign in.
  • No ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Your setup is saved on your phone. We do not have a server, an account system or a database.
  • Game data goes straight from ESPN's public feeds to your phone.

What the app saves on your phone

The app saves the following on your device only:

  • The teams, players and leagues you follow
  • Your alert settings and your Watch Worthy rules
  • What stays hidden, and the games whose score you chose to show
  • Your profile: a name, a short line about you, a favorite team, and a picture if you add one
  • The look of the app: colors, background and, if you pick one, a background photo
  • Your watch list, the games you marked as watched or skipped, and your own ratings
  • Simple counts for your stats, such as verdicts seen and time saved
  • The list of alerts the app has sent you
  • The streaming services you picked and your calendar choices
  • Schedules and scores that were downloaded, so the app opens quickly

We cannot see any of this. If you back up your iPhone with iCloud or a computer, this data can be part of that backup. Apple handles backups under its own terms.

What leaves your phone

Game data. Schedules, scores, logos and player pictures are loaded by your phone directly from ESPN's public servers (site.api.espn.com, site.web.api.espn.com and a.espncdn.com). As with any request on the internet, ESPN's servers see your IP address and basic details about your device. A request says which league, team or game is being loaded. It does not contain your name or your profile. ESPN handles these requests under its own privacy policy.

Links you open. When you open a game on ESPN, or a streaming service, from the app, it opens in your browser or in that service's app. What happens there is covered by that service's own policy.

Things you share. You can share your setup as a code, a link or a QR code. It holds the teams, players and leagues you follow and, if you leave them switched on, your profile name, your alert settings, what stays hidden, and your colors and background. You can also share a verdict card, your fan card, a calendar file or a backup file. All of these go only where you send them.

Permissions

The app asks for a permission only when you use the feature that needs it. You can change each one in the Settings app of your iPhone.

  • Notifications: alerts are set on your phone by the app itself. No push service is used and no device token is collected.
  • Camera: to scan a friend's share code. Nothing the camera sees is saved or sent.
  • Photos: to pick a profile picture or a background, and to save a card when you ask. A picture you pick is copied into the app's storage on your phone.
  • Calendar: to add the games you choose to your calendar.

Analytics, ads and tracking

Gameworthy contains no analytics, no advertising and no tracking code of any kind. It does not use an advertising identifier.

Children's privacy

Gameworthy is not directed at children under 13. It does not collect personal information from anyone, of any age.

Your control

  • To erase everything, open Settings in the app, then More, then Your data, then Erase everything.
  • Deleting the app removes everything it saved on your phone.
  • There is no account to delete.
