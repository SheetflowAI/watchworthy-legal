Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 30, 2026

Watchworthy ("we", "the app") is built with privacy as a default. This document explains what data we collect, store, and share.

What we collect

The app stores the following locally on your device only:

  • Followed teams and leagues
  • Notification preferences and custom message templates
  • Profile name, emoji, photo, or selected team avatar
  • Theme settings (light/dark/system, color preset)
  • Hide-scores preference and other UI toggles

None of this data leaves your device. We do not have a server, account system, or database.

Third-party services

Game data, scores, schedules, team logos, and player headshots come from publicly available ESPN data feeds at site.api.espn.com. These requests are made anonymously from your device. We do not share your information with ESPN.

Watchworthy is not affiliated with ESPN, the NFL, NBA, MLB, NHL, or any league or team.

Push notifications

When you enable notifications, the app uses iOS local notifications. We do not collect, log, or analyze notification activity. No notifications are sent through any external server.

Analytics & tracking

Watchworthy contains no analytics, telemetry, advertising identifiers, or tracking SDKs of any kind.

Photo library access

If you choose to upload a profile photo, the app requests permission to access your photo library. The selected image is stored locally on your device only.

Children's privacy

Watchworthy does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13. The app is rated 4+ on the App Store.

Your control

You can clear all data by uninstalling the app. There is no account to delete.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept them.
